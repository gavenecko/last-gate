FIELD GUIDE

How to play

Goal

The dead come from every edge. Keep the gate in the center alive. Clear stages, pick a card after each one, spend cash in the shop, and hold out for 100 stages.

Touch and mouse

  • Drag a heroine to move her. Double-tap her to let her roam again.
  • Tap a heroine to open her card: stats, traits, outfits.
  • Tap a face in the bottom bar to fire her ability. Hold it to open her card.
  • SHOP pauses the game. Hire heroines, upgrade the gate, build defenses.
  • START WAVE sends the dead. Nothing starts until you press it.

Difficulty

Pick Easy, Normal or Hard on the title screen when you start a run. Easy: weaker zombies, more cash. Hard: tougher zombies, extra ash. The Daily Run is always Normal.
